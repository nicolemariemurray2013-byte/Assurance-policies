## 1. Determination and documentation of acquisition and selection

The organization **determines and documents details about the acquisition and selection of all data
used in its AI systems.** No data source is used until it has an entry in §2 with every column
completed; an incomplete entry blocks use. Selection decisions — including sources considered and
**rejected** — are recorded in §5, because the reason a source was not used is as material to
assurance as the reason one was.

**Selection authority.** The Data Steward decides acquisition and selection, with Legal & Privacy
approval for rights and personal data, and AI Security Lead approval where a source is externally
controlled. The AI Development Lead may propose a source but may not approve its use.

## 4. Data rights, metadata and provenance

### 4.1 Data rights

| Source | Rights basis | Constraints that follow from the rights |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 Requirement text | Licensed or lawfully derived from published standards; internal derivative works reviewed by Legal | Standard text is not redistributed verbatim beyond licensed use |
| D-2 Evaluation corpus | Explicit contractual permission from the contributing customer, **or** full synthesis where permission was absent. Default position is prohibition | Corpus use is limited to evaluation; **no training or fine-tuning**; no redistribution; entries withdrawn on request or on contract termination |
| D-3 Synthetic / adversarial | Organization-owned | None |
| D-4 Prompts and rubrics | Organization-owned intellectual property | Not disclosed externally |
| D-5 Customer evidence | Customer-owned; we hold a processor's right to process for the purpose of the assessment only | No secondary use, no aggregation across tenants, no training use, deletion on request, export on request |
| D-6 Scan evidence | Customer-owned; read under authority the customer granted | Read-only; used only for the assessment it was collected for |
| D-7 Results | Joint — customer-owned content, our generated assessment | Customer may export; we retain as the audit record for the contracted period |
| D-9 Model access | Service terms with the model provider via Bedrock | Inference only; no extraction of weights; usage within service terms |

**Rule:** where rights are unclear, the data is **not** used. Two candidate corpus contributions were
rejected on this basis (§5).

### 4.2 Associated metadata — labelling and enhancement

Every corpus entry carries: framework, framework version, control id, requirement id, the labelled
status, both labellers' identifiers, the inter-rater agreement outcome and any adjudication, the split
assignment (validation or held-out test), the de-identification confirmation, the transforms applied
(AIMS-PRO-023), the source class, and the last-updated date. Enhancement is limited to
de-identification, format normalization and labelling — **no content is synthesized into a real entry**,
because a partly invented example would corrupt ground truth. Synthetic entries are separately marked
as such (D-3) and never presented as real.

Operational evidence carries: evidence id, version, declared content type, upload timestamp, uploader,
the controls it is linked to, integrity-check result, and — for scan evidence — the account, region,
resource identifier, API called and collection timestamp.

### 4.3 Data provenance

Provenance is recorded per the process in **AIMS-PRO-024** for every source above: origin, custody
chain, each transformation, transfers of control, and validation events. The provenance chain is
complete and verifiable for D-1 through D-8. It is **incomplete by supplier constraint for D-9** — we
cannot trace the provenance of the foundation models' training data. That limitation is recorded
explicitly rather than glossed, and it is the reason fairness is tested on our own task and outputs
rather than assumed from model documentation.

## 5. Selection decisions, including rejected sources

| Candidate source | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Historical customer assessments with explicit permission | **Selected** (D-2) | Only source with real-world requirement/evidence distribution |
| Two customer contributions without clear contractual permission | **Rejected** | Rights unclear; default is prohibition. Replaced with synthesized equivalents |
| Public web-scraped compliance documents | **Rejected** | Provenance and licensing unverifiable; quality unknown; would import unattributable bias |
| Customer evidence for corpus use, absent permission | **Rejected as a class** | Contractual commitment not to use customer data for training or corpus assembly |
| Purchased third-party compliance document set | **Rejected** | Rights chain not demonstrable to the original authors; no de-identification guarantee |
| Fine-tuning on the labelled corpus | **Rejected** | Would embed customer-derived data in weights and remove the traceable rationale the product depends on (AIMS-DOC-009 §3.1) |
| Synthetic adversarial and edge-case data | **Selected** (D-3) | Covers conditions too rare in real data to validate against |
| Special-category personal data | **Not solicited, never selected** | No requirement needs it; acquiring it would create risk with no benefit |

## 6. Review and re-determination

Acquisition and selection are re-determined: on any new data source or new use of an existing source;
on the annual representativeness review (which may require acquiring under-represented data); on a
rights or contractual change; on withdrawal of a contributor's permission; and on any finding of bias
traceable to a source. Changes are recorded in §7 and re-approved by the Data Steward and Legal &
Privacy.
