**Scope:** All obligations to report or share information about the compliance-evaluation AI system

---

## 1. Documented reporting obligations

The organization **documents its obligations for reporting information about the AI system, including
what information is required, to which interested parties, and within what timeframe.** Every obligation
in §2 records all three, plus its legal or contractual basis, the responsible role, the channel and the
evidence retained. An obligation without a named owner and a stated deadline is not usable during an
incident, so both are mandatory columns.

**How obligations are identified.** Legal & Privacy maintain this register from: the jurisdictions in
which the organization operates and in which assessed organizations are established; the data-protection
regimes applicable to the personal data that may appear in evidence; sectoral regulation applying to
assessed organizations where our output is used in their regulatory reporting; customer contracts, DPAs
and usage agreements; certification and audit scheme rules; supplier terms; and internal governance
requirements. The register is re-derived on any change to intended purpose, geography, customer base or
regulatory landscape, and reviewed semi-annually regardless.

## 3. Ensuring information is shared as required by jurisdiction

The organization **ensures information is shared with the relevant interested parties as required by the
jurisdictions in which it operates.** Mechanisms, not intentions:

| Mechanism | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Pre-identified authorities and deadlines** | Each obligation names the authority and the deadline in advance (§2). Nothing is researched for the first time during an incident |
| **Jurisdiction mapping** | Legal & Privacy maintain the mapping of processing locations, customer establishments, and the obligations each triggers. Where an assessed organization is in a jurisdiction with additional duties, those duties are attached to that customer's record |
| **Trigger wiring** | Incident severity declaration (AIMS-PLAN-016 §B.2) automatically raises the obligation-review step with Legal & Privacy; a Severity 1 cannot be closed without a recorded obligations determination — including a recorded determination that none applied |
| **Shortest-deadline rule** | Where several jurisdictions apply, the **shortest** deadline governs the whole response |
| **Preliminary filing permitted, delay not** | A required notification is never delayed for completeness; unknowns are labelled and a follow-up filed |
| **Exercise** | Deadlines and contacts are verified at each semi-annual incident tabletop (most recently 2026-02-19, which tested the 72-hour clock end to end) |
| **Evidence retained** | Every discharged obligation is recorded with what was sent, to whom, by whom and when, retained 7 years per AIMS-STD-013 §4.2 — so compliance with an obligation is itself provable |
| **Escalation on doubt** | If it is unclear whether an obligation applies, it is escalated to the Chief AI Officer and the default is to notify |
| **Register maintenance** | Semi-annual review; immediate review on a new jurisdiction, a new customer sector, a change of intended purpose, or a regulatory change. Changes are approved by the AIGB |
