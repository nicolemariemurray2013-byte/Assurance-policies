# AI Roles and Responsibilities Policy

**Document ID:** AIMS-POL-003
**Version:** 2.1
**Owner:** Chief AI Officer (CAIO)
**Approved by:** Executive Leadership Team, on the recommendation of the AI Governance Board
**Approval date:** 2026-02-10
**Next scheduled review:** 2026-12-31 (annual, and on any material change)
**Scope:** All AI systems developed, deployed, operated or procured by the organization,
including the Amazon Bedrock agents and Bedrock AgentCore runtimes operated in AWS account
471885797689 (us-west-2).

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines and allocates the roles, responsibilities and accountabilities for
artificial intelligence within the organization's AI Management System (AIMS). It exists so that
every phase of the AI system life cycle has a single named accountable role, and so that no
AI-related decision — from data acquisition through retirement — is unowned.

This policy is a mandatory instrument of the AI Policy (AIMS-POL-001) and is allocated according
to the needs, size, structure and risk profile of the organization.

## 2. Policy statements

The organization **shall** maintain a documented allocation of AI roles and responsibilities.
The organization **shall** review that allocation at least annually and whenever the AI system
inventory, the organization's structure, or the AI risk register changes materially. The
organization **shall NOT** deploy any AI system into production without a named Accountable Owner
recorded in the AI System Inventory.

## 3. Defined roles, responsibilities and authority

### 3.1 AI Governance Board (AIGB)
- **Responsibility:** Owns the AI Management System. Approves the AI Policy, this policy, the AI
  objectives, and the organization's stated AI risk appetite.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the Executive Leadership Team for the effectiveness of the
  AIMS.
- **Authority:** May halt or suspend any AI system deployment or operation.
- **Membership:** CAIO (chair), CISO, Chief Privacy Officer, General Counsel, VP Engineering,
  Responsible AI Lead.
- **Cadence:** Quarterly, and on demand for any high-impact AI system decision.

### 3.2 Chief AI Officer (CAIO)
- **Responsibility:** Develops, reviews and evaluates the AI Policy and this roles policy;
  maintains the AI System Inventory; reports AIMS performance to the AIGB.
- **Accountability:** Single management-approved role accountable for AI governance overall.
- **Authority:** Approves AI system impact assessments; assigns Accountable Owners.

### 3.3 Responsible AI Lead
- **Responsibility:** Owns the responsible-AI objectives (fairness, transparency,
  explainability, human oversight, safety) and the AI system impact assessment process.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the CAIO for responsible-AI outcomes across the portfolio.

### 3.4 AI System Accountable Owner (per system)
- **Responsibility:** Named, individual accountability for one AI system across its entire life
  cycle. Maintains that system's technical documentation, intended-use statement, impact
  assessment and risk treatment.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the CAIO. Recorded per system in the AI System Inventory.
- **Current allocation:** the Bedrock agent and Bedrock AgentCore runtime assets in account
  471885797689 are allocated to the Platform Engineering Accountable Owner.

### 3.5 AI Development Lead
- **Responsibility:** Design, development, verification and validation of AI systems, including
  model selection, evaluation and documented testing before release.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the AI System Accountable Owner.

### 3.6 Data Steward
- **Responsibility:** Provenance, lineage, quality, acquisition legality and retention/deletion
  of all data used for AI development, training, tuning and evaluation.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the AI System Accountable Owner, and jointly to the Chief
  Privacy Officer for personal data.

### 3.7 AI Operations Lead (SRE)
- **Responsibility:** Deployment, monitoring, event logging, alerting, incident detection and
  performance/drift monitoring of AI systems in production.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the AI System Accountable Owner.

### 3.8 AI Security Lead
- **Responsibility:** Threat modelling for AI systems, adversarial and prompt-injection testing,
  access control, secrets handling, and AI security incident response.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the CISO.

### 3.9 AI Risk Manager
- **Responsibility:** Maintains the AI risk register; ensures every identified AI-related risk has
  an owner, a treatment and a review date; escalates risks that exceed the approved risk appetite.
- **Accountability:** Accountable to the AIGB.

### 3.10 Internal Audit
- **Responsibility:** Independent assurance over the AIMS. Reports findings directly to the AIGB.
- **Accountability:** Independent of all roles above; does not own any AI system.

### 3.11 All personnel
- **Responsibility:** Comply with the AI Policy and report AI-related concerns through the channels
  in the Reporting of Concerns Procedure (AIMS-PRO-004), including anonymously and without
  retaliation.

## 4. Accountability across the AI system life cycle

Every life-cycle phase has exactly one accountable role. This mapping is the organization's
allocation of accountability and is reviewed with this policy.

| # | AI system life-cycle phase | Accountable role | Consulted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inception / intended use definition | AI System Accountable Owner | Responsible AI Lead, Legal |
| 2 | AI impact assessment | Responsible AI Lead | CAIO, Chief Privacy Officer |
| 3 | Risk identification and treatment | AI Risk Manager | AI Security Lead, AIGB |
| 4 | Data acquisition, provenance and preparation | Data Steward | Chief Privacy Officer, Legal |
| 5 | Design and development | AI Development Lead | AI Security Lead |
| 6 | Verification and validation (evaluation) | AI Development Lead | Responsible AI Lead |
| 7 | Release approval / deployment authorization | AI System Accountable Owner | AIGB (high impact) |
| 8 | Deployment | AI Operations Lead | AI Development Lead |
| 9 | Operation, event logging and monitoring | AI Operations Lead | AI Security Lead |
| 10 | Incident detection, response and communication | AI Security Lead | AI Operations Lead, Legal |
| 11 | Change management and re-evaluation | AI Development Lead | AI System Accountable Owner |
| 12 | Third-party / supplier oversight | AI System Accountable Owner | Procurement, Legal |
| 13 | Decommissioning, data deletion and retirement | Data Steward | AI Operations Lead |
| 14 | Independent assurance | Internal Audit | AIGB |

## 5. Basis of the allocation

The allocation above is not generic; it is derived from three documented inputs, and it is
re-derived whenever any of them changes:

1. **The organization's AI policies.** Each role's remit maps to a commitment in the AI Policy
   (AIMS-POL-001) — human oversight, transparency, data protection, security, and continual
   improvement of the AIMS.
2. **The organization's AI objectives.** Each responsible-AI objective in the AI Objectives
   Register (AIMS-REG-002) names one accountable role from section 3, so no objective is unowned.
3. **The identified AI-related risks.** Every risk in the AI Risk Register (AIMS-REG-001) is
   assigned to a role in section 3. The current mapping is:

| Identified AI risk | Accountable role |
|---|---|
| R-01 Harmful, biased or discriminatory model output | Responsible AI Lead |
| R-02 Prompt injection / adversarial manipulation of an agent | AI Security Lead |
| R-03 Leakage of personal or confidential data via an AI system | Chief Privacy Officer |
| R-04 Unlogged or untraceable AI decision-making | AI Operations Lead |
| R-05 Model or data drift degrading performance outside intended conditions | AI Development Lead |
| R-06 Use of an AI system outside its documented intended use | AI System Accountable Owner |
| R-07 Unlawful or unlicensed training-data acquisition | Data Steward |
| R-08 Over-reliance on a third-party model provider | AI System Accountable Owner |
| R-09 Loss of availability of a production AI system | AI Operations Lead |
| R-10 Insufficient human oversight of automated decisions | Responsible AI Lead |

Segregation of duties is enforced: the role that develops and validates an AI system (3.5) is
never the role that grants its production release authorization (3.4), and Internal Audit (3.10)
owns no AI system.

## 6. Communication

This policy is published on the internal AIMS portal, is mandatory reading in onboarding for all
engineering and data personnel, and is summarized for external interested parties in the public
Responsible AI Statement. Role assignments are recorded per system in the AI System Inventory and
are visible to all personnel.
